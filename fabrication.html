Master Craftsmanship In Action

HOW WE BUILD YOUR CUSTOM TRAILER

Step inside our Panama City fabrication facility. Experience how raw aerospace-grade aluminum and galvanized steel become the safest marine trailer on the road.

Stage 01: Engineering Phase
Laser Strake Mapping: Translating hull geometry into CNC-bent bunk uprights with 0% hull point load.
Precision Hull Cradle Engineering

3D Hull Measurement & CAD Load Modeling

Every custom trailer starts with millimeter-accurate data. We analyze your vessel's deadrise angle, stepped hull vents, transducer pockets, and shaft/rudder clearances to design a custom 3D cradle in SolidWorks.

✓

Center-of-Gravity (CoG) Weight Calculation

Calculates fuel load, outboard engine weights, and gear distribution to place axles for ideal tongue weight.

✓

Ramp Launch Clearance & Draft Optimization

Engineered frame cross-members maintain lowest possible center of gravity for effortless shallow water launching.

✓

Structural FEA Stress Analysis

Simulates high-speed highway shock, emergency braking, and pothole impacts before metal is cut.

Typical Engineering Turnaround: 48 Hours
Stage 02: Structural Welding Phase
AWS Certified Welding: Structural full-penetration welding of 6061-T6 aluminum I-beams & tubular tongue.
Aerospace Metal Craftsmanship

AWS TIG/MIG Structural Fabrication

Our welders hold AWS D1.1 (Structural Steel) and AWS D1.2 (Structural Aluminum) certifications. We cut main frame rails from extruded 6061-T6 marine aluminum I-beams on our heavy-duty CNC plasma table.

✓

Gusseted Aluminum Cross-Members

Cold-formed v-bend cross members provide natural keel clearance without sacrificing rigidity.

✓

Continuous Full-Penetration Welds

Zero stitch welds on stress points. Every seam is continuously welded to lock out saltwater ingress.

✓

Stainless Steel 316 Hardware Rigging

All structural bolts, u-bolts, and bunk fasteners are military-grade 316 stainless with nylon locknuts.

Certified Standard: AWS D1.2 Master Spec
Stage 03: Running Gear Phase
Running Gear Installation: Independent rubber torsion axles with vented dacromet disc brakes.
Heavy-Duty Running Gear & Braking

Torsion Axles, Surge Brakes & Hub Assembly

A trailer is only as good as what contacts the highway. We equip every custom build with genuine Dexter Torflex® or heavy-duty galvanized multi-leaf suspension paired with stainless steel disc brakes.

✓

Independent Rubber Torsion Suspension

Absorbs 80% more road shock than leaf springs, reducing boat bounce and fatigue on long highway hauls.

✓

All-Wheel Vented Disc Brake Systems

Dacromet-coated or full stainless steel calipers with ceramic pads for rapid heat dissipation and zero saltwater seizing.

✓

Vault® Hybrid Oil / Super Lube Marine Hubs

Pressurized bearing lubrication eliminates air pockets, preventing water intrusion upon ramp submergence.

Suspension Options: Torsion or Leaf Springs
Stage 04: Final Rigging & Test
Final Quality Certification: IP68 fully submersible LED wiring, carpeted cypress bunks, laser track test.
Rigging, Testing & Road Readiness

Submersible LED Rigging & Load Testing

Before any trailer leaves our workshop, it undergoes a comprehensive 40-point quality audit. We wire our lighting harnesses internally inside the aluminum I-beams with zero exposed splices.

✓

Sealed IP68 Submersible LED Lighting

Epoxy-encapsulated electronics with continuous copper ground lines to avoid frame corrosion faults.

✓

Marine Cypress Bunks & UV Polyurethane Sliders

Rot-resistant southern yellow pine/cypress wrapped in marine-grade 20-oz UV carpet for non-scratch hull boarding.

✓

Laser Axle Alignment & Dynamic Pull Test

Tested to guarantee zero tire scrub and 100% straight tracking under full simulated vessel payload.

Inspection: 40-Point Signoff

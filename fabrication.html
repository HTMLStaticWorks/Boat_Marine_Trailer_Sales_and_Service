CUSTOM TRAILER
FABRICATION

When off-the-shelf won't cut it. We design, engineer, and fabricate custom boat trailers perfectly matched to your vessel's hull geometry and weight distribution.

THE CUSTOM ADVANTAGE

Custom Frame Design

We utilize CNC plasma cutting to fabricate frames out of heavy-duty galvanized steel or marine-grade aluminum, ensuring structural integrity that far exceeds generic models.

Perfect Hull Support

A custom bunk layout prevents hull deformation. We take precise 3D measurements of your boat to build a cradle that supports it exactly where the manufacturer intended.

Axle Configuration

Whether you need a heavy-duty single axle for tight launches or a commercial-grade quad-axle setup for a 45-ft yacht, we calculate the exact axle placement for ideal tongue weight.

HOW WE BUILD IT

1

Consultation & Measurement

We analyze your boat's specs, your tow vehicle, and how you plan to use the trailer.

2

Engineering & Design

Our team creates a CAD model detailing load distribution, cross-member placement, and axle geometry.

3

Raw Fabrication

AWS-certified welders construct the frame. Steel frames are then sent for hot-dip galvanizing.

4

Assembly & Finishing

Axles, brakes, bunks, and lighting are installed. The trailer undergoes a full load and alignment test.

About Our Company

THE MARINEHAULPRO
STORY

Eighteen years of building the trailers that get serious boaters on the water — and back home safely. We're not a big box retailer. We're a specialist workshop built around one passion: marine trailer excellence.

OUR STORY

MarineHaul Pro was founded in 2006 by Marcus Delray — a former marine engineer and avid offshore angler who grew frustrated with the lack of quality boat trailer options available to serious boaters. The trailers on the market were either built to a price point, not a performance standard, or required major modifications to fit real boats properly.

Starting with a 2,400 sq ft workshop in Panama City, Florida and three certified welders, Marcus began building galvanized steel trailers to order. Word spread quickly through the local fishing and boating community. By 2010, the business had outgrown two facilities and now occupies a purpose-built 8,400 sq ft marine trailer manufacturing and service center.

Today, MarineHaul Pro ships custom trailers across the Southeast and maintains a fleet of 40+ trailers for its commercial charter operator clients. Every trailer still leaves our floor with the same personal inspection that Marcus performed on trailer number one.

WHAT WE BUILD

Our product philosophy is simple: build every trailer as if it's going to haul the most valuable boat in existence. Because to our clients, it is.

Single Axle Trailers

Lightweight, maneuverable, and purpose-built for boats under 22 ft. Our single axle designs maximize fuel efficiency and ramp access.

Tandem Axle Trailers

The workhorse configuration for 22–32 ft boats. Superior load stability, hydraulic surge brakes, and marine-grade galvanized frames as standard.

Custom Fabricated Trailers

For boats that don't fit standard configurations. We design and build from raw material, custom to your boat's hull profile, weight, and use case.

MARINE-GRADE STANDARDS

Every material and component on a MarineHaul Pro trailer is selected for long-term performance in saltwater and freshwater environments. We don't compromise on corrosion resistance — it's the single biggest predictor of trailer longevity.

A36 Structural Steel — Hot-Dip Galvanized

ASTM A36 steel forms our primary frames. Every unit is hot-dip galvanized to ASTM A123 specification — minimum 3.5 oz/ft² zinc coating.

Dexter & EZ-Lube Axle Systems

We use only Dexter Axle-brand spindles and EZ-Lube bearing protectors on every unit. No off-brand axle hardware — period.

Submersible LED Lighting — IP68 Rated

Every trailer ships with IP68-rated fully submersible LED tail lights. 80,000-hour rated lifespan — built for repeated boat ramp submersion.

5-Year Structural Warranty

We warrant every frame and structural weld for 5 years against manufacturing defects. Our confidence in our craftsmanship is in writing.

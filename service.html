OUR SERVICE PROCESS

Transparent, efficient, and professional from drop-off to pick-up.

1

Drop-Off & Inspection

Bring your trailer in (with or without the boat). Our technicians will perform a thorough visual and mechanical inspection to identify all issues.

2

Diagnosis & Estimate

We provide a fully itemized written estimate detailing exactly what needs repair, the parts required, and the labor cost. No surprise bills.

3

Approval

You review the estimate and authorize the work. We do not begin any repairs without your explicit go-ahead.

4

Expert Repair

Our certified mechanics perform the repairs using OEM or superior marine-grade parts in our specialized service bays.

5

Quality Assurance Check

A senior technician reviews all work performed, torque-checks all critical fasteners, and tests lighting and brake operation.

6

Ready for Pickup

You are notified that your trailer is ready. You leave with the confidence that your equipment is safe, reliable, and road-ready.

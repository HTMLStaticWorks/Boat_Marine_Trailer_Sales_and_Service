HOW WE BUILD IT

Marine trailer engineering is not a commodity. Every structural decision we make is driven by physics, load science, and long-term corrosion resistance.

01

Marine-Grade Material Selection

We use only hot-dipped galvanized A36 structural steel and 6061-T6 marine aluminum alloy. Both resist saltwater corrosion without compromising tensile strength.

02

CNC Precision Fabrication

Every frame member is plasma-cut with CNC accuracy. Cross members are positioned by load-bearing calculations, not approximation. Tolerances held to ±0.5mm.

03

Certified Welding & Assembly

All structural welds performed by AWS-certified welders. MIG welding on galvanized steel; TIG on aluminum. Full penetration welds on all load-bearing joints.

04

Axle Alignment & Load Testing

Every trailer leaves our floor with certified axle alignment and a load test at 110% rated capacity. We document and retain all test results with each unit.

MARINE OWNER PRO TIPS

Expert knowledge from our service team to help you protect your trailer investment.

🔩

Repack Bearings Annually

Saltwater submersion accelerates grease breakdown. Repack or replace wheel bearings at least once per year — every 6 months for saltwater use.

💡

Upgrade to LED Lighting

Submersible LED tail lights last 10x longer than incandescent in marine environments. A $120 upgrade that prevents $800 in roadside failures.

⚖️

Check Tongue Weight

Tongue weight should be 10-15% of total trailer + boat weight. Too little causes dangerous sway. Too much overloads your tow vehicle hitch.

🚿

Rinse After Every Launch

A 60-second freshwater rinse after saltwater use removes the chloride that destroys galvanized steel. Simple habit, extends trailer life by years.
